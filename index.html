Pablo Rivero - Designer · Artist · Curator

INFO

Pablo Rivero (Stipinpixel) is a visual artist, designer, curator, educator, and game developer working at the intersection of digital culture, heritage, and contemporary art. His practice explores pixel systems, digital archaeology, speculative interfaces, and game-based environments as tools for rethinking memory, territory, and cultural narratives. Through pixel art, interactive experiences, generative processes, and hybrid physical-digital works, he investigates how local histories and global digital cultures can coexist and transform each other. Based in Tucumán, Argentina, he is the founder of Maskay Gallery, a platform dedicated to supporting emerging artists from Northwest Argentina through digital art, Web3, and collaborative practices. His work has been exhibited internationally in Argentina, Japan, Switzerland, Canada, the United States, Spain, and Belgium.

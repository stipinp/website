Archaeological Research

Everything you encounter within Suri Dimension is grounded in rigorous, real-world research. Suri Dimension is a space where matter vibrates and meaning becomes magic.


Throughout this dimension, you will find words in Quechua, the native language of the Yungas Biosphere in Northwest Argentina—an active archaeological research territory. Characters emerge from ancestral iconographies, figurines, ceramics, and archaeological artifacts, while landscapes are shaped by the region’s natural and geological heritage.


What is Suri Dimension? Enjoy the adventure alongside Yukk.
